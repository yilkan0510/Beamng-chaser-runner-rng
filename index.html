Pace score (0–100) blends the official 0–100 km/h time (half the weight), top speed (30%) and power-to-weight (20%). A gap of 3–8 points is roughly a trim level or one tune step. Times marked ~ are estimated from power-to-weight because the game's test never reached 100 km/h. In a lobby, these rules, names and rolls are shared by everyone.
